## Selected work

### [OpenAMP Foundry](https://github.com/Open-Problem-Lab/OpenAMP-Foundry)

A dry-lab pipeline for ranking antimicrobial peptide candidates. It combines transparent baselines, cluster-aware evaluation, computational safety screens, novelty checks, and auditable evidence certificates. Computation can prioritize experiments; it cannot establish biological activity, selectivity, or safety.

### [Mispricing Desk](https://mispricing-desk.4rqnf2gvxf.workers.dev/)

A public research desk tracking the gap between market narrative and primary evidence. The archive holds more than 500 notes; current screens focus on U.S. and Asian equities, using filings, company disclosures, and market data. Recent notes isolate one priced narrative and test it against one competing factor.

### [Lumi](https://runlumi.app/)

Lumi turns scattered team updates into a short daily brief for small-business owners: decisions waiting, overdue work, and risks that need attention. I build and operate its mobile, web, backend, and release systems.

### [ClawPing](https://github.com/cschanhniem/clawping)

ClawPing monitors home servers behind CGNAT. A static Go agent sends health checks outbound to a Cloudflare control plane, which tracks incidents and alerts through Telegram. It needs no inbound ports, dynamic DNS, or monitoring VPS.
